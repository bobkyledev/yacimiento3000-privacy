1. How to request it

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate.
  • By email: write to bobkyledev@gmail.com from your account email, with the subject "Delete account Digcremental". We delete it within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

2. What is deleted

  • Your account (email and user ID).
  • Your app data stored on our servers, such as your progress.
  • Your devices' notification tokens.

3. What is kept

  • Crashlytics crash reports, which do not include your name or email; Google keeps them for up to 90 days.
  • Aggregated usage statistics, which do not identify you.
  • Nothing else.
