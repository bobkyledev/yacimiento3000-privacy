Privacy Policy

Digcremental

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Español · English

Digcremental is an app by Bob & Kyle. This policy explains what data it collects, why, and who it is shared with.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: if you sign in with Google, Apple or email, we store your email and a user ID through Firebase Authentication. We use them to save your progress and restore it on other devices.
  • App data: we store in Google Cloud Firestore the data the app needs to work (for example, your progress), linked to your user ID.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, we store your device notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send them.

2. Third-party services

These providers process data so the app can work, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics and App Check).
  • Google and Apple, if you sign in with their accounts.
  • Google AdMob, to show ads.

3. Performance and crashes

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes. If the app closes unexpectedly, a technical report is sent that does not include your name or email:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • The point in the code where the error happened.

Firebase App Check verifies that requests come from the genuine app.

4. Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and improve it. We do not send your email to analytics.

Where the law requires consent (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), analytics only starts if you accept.

5. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID.

Before showing ads we ask for consent where the law requires it and, on iPhone, for Apple's tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). Personalised ads depend on that choice, which you can change in the app or device settings.

6. Local storage

We store some preferences on your device (for example, the language or whether you have seen the intro). They never leave the device.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • By email, writing to bobkyledev@gmail.com.

More details at https://bobkyledev.github.io/yacimiento3000-privacy/delete-account/en/

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date.

10. Contact

For any question about this policy, write to bobkyledev@gmail.com.
